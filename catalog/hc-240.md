# HC-240 cabinet and door parts

The HC-240 is an upright refrigerated display cabinet sold through dealers. The door assembly has two revisions. The `GS-240-A` gasket fits the original Rev A channel. The `GS-240-B` gasket fits the later Rev B channel. The model and serial number alone do not establish which door assembly is fitted, because doors may have been replaced in service. A clear door-label or gasket-channel photo is needed to confirm the revision before recommending a kit.

Condensation at the door edge can have several causes, including a damaged gasket, incomplete closure, frequent opening, or a temperature difference. The reported timing after cleaning does not identify a cause. If the cabinet is failing to hold a safe temperature, dealer staff should follow their own food-safety and equipment-escalation procedures; this source does not provide a diagnosis or repair instruction.
