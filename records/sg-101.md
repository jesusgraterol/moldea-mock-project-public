# SG-101: Bike commute with a laptop

A shopper carries a 16-inch laptop and lunch on a 12 km bike commute. They sometimes ride in rain and ask which bag to consider. They have not given a budget or asked to buy today.

The shop wants a concise recommendation based on the catalog snapshot, with useful tradeoffs and a clear distinction between the bag's fabric treatment and an included cover. The assistant cannot confirm fit for an exact laptop model, weatherproofing, current stock, or delivery.
