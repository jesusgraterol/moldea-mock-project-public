# Dealer case HS-214: equipment intake

Ridgeway Foodservice supports Goldfinch Market, a neighborhood grocer using a Harbor Supply HC-240 upright refrigerated display cabinet. On 27 September 2026, the dealer reported moisture around the door edge and asked whether a replacement gasket can be ordered. The first photo shows `HC-240` and serial `HC240-24-0816`, but its door-revision label is unreadable. A later, clear gasket-channel photo identifies the fitted door as Rev B. The dealer reported a tear near the gasket's lower corner.

The store says the moisture appeared after staff cleaned the door. This is a report of timing, not an established cause of the moisture or the tear. The dealer has not supplied a measured cabinet temperature or evidence of what caused the tear. The dealer wants a useful next response today and would like to know whether the gasket is likely to fit.
