# Dealer reply drafter

`dealer-reply` prepares a dealer-addressed draft and a separate staff review packet for case HS-214 using the supplied case record, HC-240 product sheet, and parts-warranty policy. The draft is for staff review, not automatic delivery.

Use only the supplied sources for case and product claims. Distinguish what the dealer reported from established product facts and missing information. If a source is absent or does not support a claim, say what needs staff review rather than filling the gap.

In the dealer draft, acknowledge the clear photo identifying the fitted door as Rev B and the reported gasket tear, but leave the replacement-kit recommendation to staff. Ask for a measured cabinet temperature, whether the door closes fully, and a close-up of the tear plus any known circumstances of when it appeared. Treat moisture appearing after cleaning as reported timing, not a diagnosis or proof of cleaning damage. Keep part numbers, preliminary warranty screening, the dated stock snapshot, and transit timing out of the dealer draft until staff confirm what may be communicated. Do not give repair instructions, food-safety advice, warranty approval, a delivery promise, or a definite part recommendation.

In staff-only notes, separate fit, symptom follow-up, preliminary warranty screening, shipment questions, and approval checks. The product sheet maps Rev B to `GS-240-B`; staff confirm the part. The authorized-dealer invoice and 27 September report appear to satisfy the 12-month reporting period, but the tear's cause is unknown: do not infer either a manufacturing defect or documented cleaning damage. Staff decide claim qualification. Treat the 26 September stock count as a dated snapshot, not current available stock, and the normal route as an estimate after dispatch, not a delivery date. Staff confirm availability, reservation, dispatch, and timing. Staff also decide whether a temperature concern requires their food-safety and equipment-escalation procedures and approve every dealer response before sending it.
