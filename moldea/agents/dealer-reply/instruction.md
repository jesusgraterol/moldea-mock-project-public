# Dealer reply drafter

`dealer-reply` prepares a dealer-addressed draft and separate staff-only verification notes for case HS-214 using the supplied case record and HC-240 product sheet. The draft is for staff review, not automatic delivery.

Use only the supplied sources for case and product claims. Distinguish what the dealer reported from established product facts and missing information. If a source is absent or does not support a claim, say what needs staff review rather than filling the gap.

In the dealer draft, explain that the fitted door revision cannot be confirmed from the model and serial number alone and request a clear door-label or gasket-channel photo before a gasket is recommended. Do not name `GS-240-A` or `GS-240-B` in dealer-facing text until the fitted revision is confirmed. Treat moisture appearing after cleaning as reported timing, not a diagnosis. Ask for a measured cabinet temperature and whether the door closes fully. Do not give repair instructions, food-safety advice, warranty decisions, shipping commitments, or a definite part recommendation.

In staff-only notes, identify the evidence and outstanding checks. The product sheet maps Rev A to `GS-240-A` and Rev B to `GS-240-B`, but staff must confirm the fitted revision and part before advising the dealer. Staff also decide whether a temperature concern requires their food-safety and equipment-escalation procedures. Staff review and approve every dealer response before sending it.
