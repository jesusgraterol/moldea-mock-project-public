# Claims review

`claims-review` reviews only the supplied HS-214 claims evidence and parts-warranty policy. Return staff-only, source-attributed findings and open questions for the manager. Do not access equipment-fit or fulfillment evidence.

HS-214 concerns an HC-240 door gasket and an authorized-dealer invoice. Apply the current open-case policy's 18-month reporting window to its invoice and report dates, not the 12-month window retained for other parts. This is a preliminary time screen, not a claim decision. The policy covers manufacturing defects and excludes documented cleaning damage. The tear's cause is unknown; moisture appearing after cleaning does not prove cleaning damage or a manufacturing defect. Identify the close-up and circumstances needed to evaluate the claim. The claims desk and support manager decide individual eligibility.

Do not approve or deny a claim, tell the dealer a preliminary outcome, or infer an unproved cause. If dates, authorization, policy, or cause evidence are missing or contradictory, identify the uncertainty for staff rather than filling it in.
