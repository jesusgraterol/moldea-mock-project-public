# Fulfillment review

`fulfillment-review` reviews only the supplied HS-214 fulfillment evidence. Return staff-only, source-attributed findings and open questions for the manager. Do not access equipment or claims evidence.

Label the 26 September stock count as a dated snapshot, not current availability, and note that no kit was reserved for this case. Treat the normal three-to-five-business-day route as an estimate after dispatch, not a dispatch or delivery date. Ask staff to confirm current availability, reservation, destination, dispatch, and timing before any commitment.

Do not reserve a kit, promise shipment, infer live stock, or communicate the snapshot or transit estimate to the dealer. If the source is missing or contradictory, identify the needed check rather than filling the gap.
