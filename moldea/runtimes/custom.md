# HS-214 custom runtime

The repository owns source-only custom invocations. `createEquipmentSupportAgent`, `createClaimsReviewAgent`, and `createFulfillmentReviewAgent` each load their own canonical instruction and only their desk-owned checked-in sources, then pass those texts to an injected review invoker. The `runHs214Preview` workflow waits for all three source-attributed, staff-only reviews before calling `createDealerReplyAgent`. The writer loads its own canonical instruction through `loadDealerReplyInstruction` and receives the reviews, not the underlying source texts. The staff CLI binds deterministic case-specific previews and prints each desk's findings and open questions separately from the dealer draft and manager checks. Warranty-window screening, source-snapshot checks, and sequencing are deterministic; staff retain the decisions.

Each desk preview is tied to reviewed source and instruction snapshots, and the writer preview is tied to its instruction and the three reviewed outputs. They fail rather than presenting fixed wording after those inputs change. Typed source restriction is inspectable wiring, not an OS-enforced isolation boundary. The packet is not a model response and does not call a provider, reserve a part, decide a claim, send a message, persist a draft, or make a manager decision. No provider implementation, delivery path, or deployed service is integrated; each agent's unresolved model-execution requirement records that limit.
