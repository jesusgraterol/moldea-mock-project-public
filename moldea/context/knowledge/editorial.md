# Knowledge ownership

This document describes the fictional Parcel Desk explorer fixture.

Canonical context owns durable project rules. Proposed knowledge edits cite their source and remain drafts. Historical decisions retain their status; a rejected or superseded decision cannot become current policy by being linked.
