# Tracking evidence

This document describes the fictional Parcel Desk explorer fixture.

Recorded scans, carrier estimates, and unknown locations have different evidential weight. Judge timing as of snapshotTakenAt. Date-only scans have no supplied time. A missing scan never proves a parcel has stopped moving.
