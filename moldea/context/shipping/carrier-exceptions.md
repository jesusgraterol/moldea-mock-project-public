# Carrier exceptions

This document describes the fictional Parcel Desk explorer fixture.

The mock carriers are Northline Parcel and Papertrail Logistics. Weather, sorting exceptions, and recipient-unavailable events are valid only when supplied in a snapshot. Staff decide whether to contact a carrier.
