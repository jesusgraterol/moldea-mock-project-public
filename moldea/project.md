# Incident Desk

Incident Desk is a source-only prototype for on-call engineers at a fictional appointment-booking platform. It classifies supplied alert signals and drafts short handoff briefs from classifications for human incident review.

Keep source observations and raw event IDs inspectable. Distinguish observed measurements and deployment timing from hypotheses about impact or cause, and keep model-drafted synopsis text separate from source evidence. Engineers decide whether to page, declare an incident, or change production; the prototype makes none of those decisions and takes no operational action.
