# Harbor Supply

Harbor Supply is a fictional dealer-support team for refrigerated display equipment. This repository is a source-only prototype for preparing a useful, evidence-grounded review packet before staff respond to a dealer. It serves the equipment, claims, and fulfillment reviewers and the manager who approves the response.

For case HS-214, the checked-in case record, HC-240 catalog entry, claims supplement, fulfillment snapshot, and parts-warranty policy provide separate evidence for equipment fit, reported symptoms, claim eligibility, and shipment facts. A report or snapshot is not a confirmed diagnosis, approved claim, available part, or shipping commitment. Reviewers must preserve missing evidence and uncertainty.

The manager makes the final decision on any dealer response and every part, claim, shipping, or safety commitment. The prototype requires no working service, deployment, credentials, live provider call, or dealer delivery.
