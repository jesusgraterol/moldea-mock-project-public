# Catalog Studio

Catalog Studio is a source-only prototype for a fictional home-goods shop. It helps merchandisers draft product descriptions from local fact sheets. A staff editor reviews every draft before storefront use.

The checked-in fact sheets cover the Harbor Linen Throw (CS-214) and Glaze Stoneware Mug (CS-215). They include confirmed facts alongside vendor slogans or proposed marketing lines. The throw has no emissions evidence on file.

This repository does not require a working service, deployment, credentials, live provider call, or storefront publication.
