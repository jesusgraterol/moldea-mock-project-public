# Parcel Desk

Parcel Desk helps support staff at a small online stationery shop draft short shipment explanations from caller-supplied tracking snapshots. The draft must distinguish recorded carrier scans from delivery estimates and unknowns, without inventing a current location or promising delivery.

Staff, not the assistant, handle refunds, reroutes, and carrier contact. The repository is a source-only prototype: it does not send customer replies and requires no working service, deployment, credentials, or live provider execution.
